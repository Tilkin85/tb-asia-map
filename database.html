Qingshui Zhongshe Human Burial Dataset

This bilingual dataset is derived from the 2024 final report for the ROC Year 109 (2020) Qingshui Zhongshe archaeological human-remains curation project. It combines 38 complete burial records documented individually in the report with 15 complete burial summaries reproduced from earlier curation projects.

Structured fields retain both an English research translation and the original Traditional Chinese value. Source-group and printed-page fields connect each record to the report. This release translates and structures burial summaries rather than claiming a line-by-line translation of all 436 pages or reproducing the complete individual recording forms.

Source & Translation Notes

Source: 《109 年清水中社考古遺址出土人骨整理計畫：結案報告書》, commissioned by the Taichung City Cultural Heritage Department, conducted by the Institute of Archaeology at National Cheng Kung University, and dated May 17, 2024.

Page references use the printed report page numbers. Uncertainty categories such as probable, indeterminate, unobservable, and unknown are preserved. The English text is a research translation, not a substitute for checking the original report before formal publication.
