Key observations

  • Prone burial is the dominant rite in both cultural layers.
  • Recorded heads cluster toward the east-southeast, with one reversed outlier.
  • Oral-health markers and degenerative change dominate the pathology record.
  • Pottery appears over the face, beneath the body, or as accompanying jars.
