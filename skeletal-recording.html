Skeletal Data Recording

A fillable digital recreation of the Arizona State Museum Bioarchaeology Laboratory Human Remains Documentation Packet (08/2024). Every recording form (1–12) is reproduced with its original outline, terminology, and scoring codes. Data stays in this browser until you export it.

Recording conventions: mark a dash (–) when not observable or not recordable. Record all measurements in millimeters. Inventory completeness codes: f = 1–25% present, p = 25–75% present, c = 75–100% present. Use “U” for unsided and “A” to denote approximated location.

6. Non-Metric Traits

General codes: 0 = absent; 1 = present/partial; 2 = complete/multiple; 9 = unobservable. Trait-specific definitions are listed in the right-hand column. L = left, M = midline, R = right.

7. Pathology Checklist

Mark present, absent, or unobservable for each region.

8. Degenerative Joint Disease

Stages of DJD (Ubelaker 1999): a = normal articular surface; b = appearance of small deposits of bone on articular margins; c = small pits; d = polishing / eburnation; e = other (describe below).

9. Spinal Osteophytosis

Osteophytosis: stages 0–4 (Ubelaker 1999). DJD: stages a–d (Ubelaker 1999).

11. Cremated Remains

Describe identifiable elements present, observations, and condition for each region.

Assessment

1. Count: number of fragments per region (exclude fragments smaller than 5 mm). 2. Weight: grams per region, then total. 3. Color (degree of burning): T tan, B black, W white/calcined, BG blue-gray; estimate percentages if mixed. 4. Texture: L longitudinally split, T longitudinal & transverse checking, C curved cracks, W warping. 5. Max. Length: maximum length (mm) of the largest fragment per region. Shading key for skeletal chart: Tan (outline), Black (fill), White/Calcined (hatch), Blue-Gray (cross-hatch).

12. Isolated Remains

List each identifiable element, indicate side and code completeness. Count and weigh remains by anatomical region and calculate totals. Describe the material and characteristics (warping, breakage, burning, etc.).

Completeness codes: C > 75%; P = 25–75%; F < 25%. Appendicular element codes: PE (proximal epiphysis), P 1/3 (proximal third of shaft), M 1/3 (middle third), D 1/3 (distal third), DE (distal epiphysis).
