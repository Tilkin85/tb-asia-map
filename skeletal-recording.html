11. Cremated Remains

Exclude fragments under 5 mm from count. Color: T tan, B black, W white/calcined, BG blue-gray; estimate percentages if mixed. Texture: L longitudinal split, T longitudinal/transverse checking, C curved cracks, W warping. Maximum length in mm.

12. Isolated Remains
