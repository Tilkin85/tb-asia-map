English source description

Final Report: 2020 Qingshui Zhongshe Archaeological Site Excavated Human Remains Curation Project. Commissioned by the Taichung City Cultural Heritage Department and conducted by the Institute of Archaeology, National Cheng Kung University. Final report dated May 17, 2024.

原始報告資訊

《109 年清水中社考古遺址出土人骨整理計畫：結案報告書》
委託單位：臺中市文化資產處
執行單位：國立成功大學考古學研究所
計畫主持人：莊家銘、趙金勇
日期：中華民國 113 年 5 月 17 日

Ethical-use note: these are records of ancestral human remains. Data should be used with care, with attention to the policies of the holding agency and the interests of descendant and Indigenous communities. No photographs of human remains are reproduced here.

5. Data Dictionary & Coding Notes

FieldMeaningNotes
source_groupOrigin of the summary record2020_curation_project = the 38 records individually documented in this report; prior_curation = 15 records summarized from earlier projects.
layerExcavation stratigraphic layerValues are transcribed exactly, such as L3a, L3e, or L4.
head_directionOrientation of the headE40°S means 40 degrees south of east. X or unknown indicates that orientation could not be established.
body / limb / arm positionBurial postureProne, supine, or side-lying; extended or flexed limbs; arm placement is retained as a separate field.
sex / ageReport assessmentProbability and observability language is preserved. Juvenile sex was generally not assessed.
pathologyObserved pathological or anomalous featuresLEH = linear enamel hypoplasia. “No obvious pathology” does not mean the individual was disease-free; it reflects what was observable.
source_report_pagesPrinted report page rangeNot the PDF viewer page number.
details_zhDetailed record textTraditional Chinese text extracted from the individual burial section, including inventory and measurement text where present. Formatting may reflect PDF extraction.
